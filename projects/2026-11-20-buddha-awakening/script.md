# Script: The Prince Who Chose Truth

Target pace: ~2.5 words/sec (~160 words, ~65 seconds).

- **l1** (Hook): Imagine having every luxury on earth... and still feeling completely empty.
- **l2** (Childhood): Twenty-five hundred years ago, a young prince named Siddhartha lived behind high palace walls.
- **l3** (Naive Prince): His father hid every trace of pain from him. He knew only music, silk, and endless spring.
- **l4** (Turning Point - Sights): Then one day, he stepped outside the gates. He saw an old man, a sick man, and a funeral.
- **l5** (Turning Point - Renunciation): For the first time, he realized wealth couldn't stop suffering. So at twenty-nine, he walked away from his crown in the dead of night.
- **l6** (Transformation): For six years, he studied with masters and starved in the forest. Until he sat beneath a Bodhi tree, and vowed not to rise until he found the truth.
- **l7** (Awakening): At dawn, his mind went completely still. The naive prince became the Buddha, the Awakened One.
- **l8** (Teaching 1 - Middle Way & Truths): He didn't preach blind faith. He taught the Middle Way, between luxury and self-harm. He taught that suffering comes from craving, and ends when we let go.
- **l9** (Teaching 2 - Mind & Compassion): Above all, he taught that peace isn't found in a palace. It's built inside your own mind, through mindfulness and compassion.
- **l10** (Payoff): You don't need to escape your life to awaken. You just need to see clearly, and begin.
