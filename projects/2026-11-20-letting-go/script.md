# Script: Letting Go

**l1** Two monks were walking by a river. They came across a woman, stuck at the bank. She was afraid of the water.

**l2** The older monk didn't say a word. He lifted her, carried her across, and set her down gently.

**l3** Then the two monks walked on. For hours. The younger one stayed silent... but his mind was loud.

**l4** Finally, he couldn't hold it in. "You carried her! We're not supposed to do that!"

**l5** The older monk smiled. "I put her down at the river. Why are you still carrying her?"

**l6** That's the whole lesson. The weight was never the woman. It was the holding.

**l7** And honestly? We do this every day, in adult life. The argument from last week. The job that ended. The friend who changed. The version of you that failed.

**l8** You already put it down, out there in the real world. But in your head... you're still carrying it.

**l9** Let it go. Not for them. For your own two hands.
