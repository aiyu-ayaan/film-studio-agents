## The Mystery Files series

Three 45 s reels, one format, so viewers learn it and come back.

Every episode has 3 parts:

| Part | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Night | about 0-10 s | Drop the viewer into the place, in the present tense. The hook is the series formula: "[Number] [people]. One [thing]. And then..." |
| 2. The Clues | about 10-27 s | Three clues, each stranger than the last. The camera walks the scene like an investigator. |
| 3. The Endgame | about 27-45 s | The big answer lands (climax), a quiet beat of doubt (aftermath), then a sting that opens the next file. |

The ending works like a blockbuster finale. The case resolves, the music drops out for one beat, then a last image and line tease what comes next.
Episode 1's sting is "Next file:" plus Episode 2's hook, word for word, and Episode 2's sting is Episode 3's hook the same way. A viewer who finishes one episode is already hooked on the next. Episode 3 ends on an open question for the next season.
The sting replaces the loop back to frame 0 that `channel/renderstand/format.md` asks for. That is the only rule this series changes.

No film footage, characters, music or names from any movie franchise. The feel is borrowed, nothing else.
